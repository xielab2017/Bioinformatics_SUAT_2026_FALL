# Homework: RNA-seq Data Analysis Using EasyMultiProfiler-Web

In your local `EasyMultiProfiler-Web` directory, locate the `tests` folder, which contains several example datasets. Identify the RNA-seq-related CSV files and upload them to **EasyMultiProfiler-Web**.

Using the RNA-seq analysis concepts and methods covered in class and Homework 1, perform a complete analysis of the selected dataset. Interpret your results and explain their biological significance based on your understanding of transcriptomics.

After completing the analysis, use the **Sync** feature in EasyMultiProfiler-Web to submit your homework to your GitHub repository. Ensure that your analysis results and relevant files have been successfully synchronized.
